Coupled head mechanism

The head uses two DM-J3507-2EC (48V) motors named head_motor_1 / 2. The motors work through the head linkage as a coordinated pair to provide pitch (look up / look down) and roll (side-to-side head tilt). Neither physical motor represents only one of these two output axes.

Coupled ankle mechanism

Each leg uses two DM-J4310P-2EC (48V) motors named left_ankle_motor_1 / 2 or right_ankle_motor_1 / 2, coupled through ball-joint connecting rods and a universal joint. This parallel ankle mechanism provides pitch (toe-up / toe-down) and roll (side-to-side foot tilt) through coordinated motion of both motors.

DM-J3507 120 Ω termination settings

DM-J3507-2EC includes a built-in 120 Ω termination switch; its default setting is OFF. All three head motors are OFF. On each wrist, the 3507 motor with CAN_ID 0x06 is OFF, and the 3507 motor with CAN_ID 0x07 is ON.

Physical switch direction

Move the built-in termination switch toward OFF or ON as shown. Verify the actual switch position before closing the robot enclosure.
